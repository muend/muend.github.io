Agent Skills · Full GeoAI Lifecycle Open sourceBenchmarked

geoai-skills

An Agent-Skills-compatible suite that turns a general AI agent into a disciplined geospatial practitioner. Eighteen specialist skills cover the full lifecycle—from STAC acquisition, PostGIS, and Earth Engine to spatial statistics, kriging, U-Net inference, cartography, and guarded ArcGIS Pro execution.

  • Encodes silent spatial failure modes: explicit CRS and units, anti-leakage defaults, accounting checks, uncertainty outputs, and visual plus numeric verification.
  • Includes an orchestrator, cross-cutting ML and software-engineering standards, typed evaluations, a deterministic benchmark harness, and CI validation.
  • Installable as a Claude plugin or as independent skills in Agent-Skills-compatible runtimes.

MCP · GeoAI InfrastructurePyPI

arcgis-mcp-bridge

A local-first Model Context Protocol framework exposing 100 specialized ArcGIS Pro and ArcPy geoprocessing tools to LLM hosts while isolating the licensed GIS runtime from the host process.

  • Async MCP server separated from a licensed ArcPy worker subprocess.
  • PathGuard validation before filesystem and geodatabase operations.
  • Mocked ArcPy testing enables development and CI without ArcGIS Pro.
Repository → PyPI →
Remote Sensing · Data PipelinePyPI · DOI

sentinel-crop-pipeline

A reproducible Sentinel-2 data-preparation pipeline for crop-classification research—from CDSE discovery and AOI-cropped download to cloud masking, spectral indices, spatially blocked patches, and aligned label masks.

  • SCL-aware invalid-pixel filtering and reflectance normalization.
  • COG, NPY, and TFRecord export for inspection and model training.
  • Spatially blocked train, validation, and test splits reduce leakage.

Spatial DSS · Public ProductLive

agri-dss

A fully client-side Spatial Decision Support System for the Western Antalya agricultural corridor—five districts and 147 neighborhoods—turning agronomic and economic knowledge into concrete, printable local plans.

  • Zero-backend static architecture over a decoupled data layer.
  • Auditable crop and regional recommendation logic.
  • Guided interface with clean A4 field-facing output.

Applied Simulation · Browser GamePublished

FOUNDER.EXE

A browser-based startup simulation modeling the pressure of founding a company in Türkiye or the USA: incorporation, taxes, grants, investor expectations, cash flow, regulation, failure, and exit outcomes.

  • Idea-aware onboarding adapts sector and market assumptions.
  • Optional Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic layer with scripted fallback.
  • Static release with local persistence and no mandatory backend.

Safety

Failure modes designed out

CRS discipline, spatial blocking, PathGuard validation, uncertainty deliverables, mutation gates, and double verification are treated as system requirements rather than optional advice.

Read design principles →
01 / Research rigor

Spatial correctness first

Projection, units, spatial dependence, validation design, and uncertainty are resolved before optimizing model scores or presentation.

02 / Engineering discipline

Reproducible by construction

Typed contracts, validation scripts, CI, immutable benchmark evidence, and modular architectures keep results reviewable and maintainable.

03 / Product thinking

Methods become usable systems

The goal is not only analysis. It is to translate complex spatial, regulatory, or economic logic into products people can actually operate.
