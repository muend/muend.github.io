PlaceTwin

A 3D digital twin and studio workspace for two urban-design sites in Ayvalık, built so that every number says where it came from and how far to trust it.

In studio usePrivate repositoryTR / EN outputs
ContextUrban design studio, İzmir Institute of Technology, Fall 2026

Tests512 for the twin · 75 for panel and site
Why

A model that admits what it does not know

Studio models usually draw every building with the same confidence. Here the height of 49 buildings comes from title deeds, 7 from OpenStreetMap, and the remaining 640 are estimates with a stated range. The twin says which is which, on every building, and a field survey promotes a building to the highest class.

Nothing in the scene is decoration. There are no representative cars, street lamps or façade materials: if it is not data, it is not drawn. Counts that depend on incomplete cadastral coverage are shown as lower bounds and labelled provisional until the coverage threshold is met.

From sources to studio

A deterministic build turns open and official sources into one data file and one summary. The panel, the site and the assistant all read that same summary, so the numbers a student sees in three places cannot disagree.

Assistant

Answers come from the twin, not from memory

The studio panel has a Claude assistant with ten tools. Eight of them read and manage the panel itself: the sixteen-week calendar, tasks, assignments, deliverables and the decision log. The other two are the grounding.

  • Building lookup. Finds up to five buildings by number, name or block and parcel, and returns area, storeys, height, the data's confidence class and source, the parcel and any field record.
  • Plan and policy lookup. Searches a sourced compilation of plans, zoning and conservation rules, decision authority, actors and disputes, and cites what it returns.

Selecting a building in the 3D scene and choosing “ask the assistant” carries that selection into the conversation as context, so the question starts from the place rather than from a description of it.

Film

Limits

An information tool, not an official plan document. Risk layers are open-data screening, not hazard maps. 640 of 696 building heights are estimates. Official data still missing: seismic design parameters, the base map, the conservation plan, the listed-building register and an approved shoreline. The repository is private and the site sits behind a login, so nothing here links out.
