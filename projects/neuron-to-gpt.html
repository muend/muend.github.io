Implementation

One chain, ten runnable stages

The project now extends beyond its original character-level GPT. Weeks 1–8 build scalar autograd, manual backpropagation, language-model layers and attention; Week 9 adds byte-level BPE and cl100k_base compatibility; Week 10 implements GPT-2 with 124,439,808 unique parameters.

The forward pass, causal attention and sampling are implemented in PyTorch. The pretrained import maps published GPT-2 weights into that implementation and checks the resulting logits against the reference, so implementation parity and training behavior have separate evidence.

Earlier milestone

The 4.8M model remains a separate result

Week 8 trained character-level models on an A100 for 5,000 updates. The Shakespeare model has 4,833,345 parameters and final validation NLL 1.4723; the Turkish model has 4,860,534 parameters and NLL 1.6239. Those validation numbers do not belong to the later 124M model.

Evidence boundary

The 124M work verifies implementation, pretrained weight import and fixed-batch training mechanics. It is not a full-corpus pretraining run or a general language-quality benchmark. GPU precision differs between the two training runs.
